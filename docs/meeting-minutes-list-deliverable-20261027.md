One starts “Prepare” and the other “Provide”. Existing bounded-survivor nomination requires the same opening operation, so neither Microsoft verification nor final proof could consider the pair. The contained-requirement comparison now has a separate generic `list_deliverable` shape. The objects must share their first two informative tokens, purposes must match, and the owner and complete source-reference sets must be identical. This is a nomination, not proof that preparation and provision are interchangeable.

Existing eligibility excludes timed, flagged, reviewer-authored, unowned, multiply-owned, rejected-date and qualified instructions. Both source reviews must establish supported commitments to the same list, scope, purpose and occurrence, plus complete retention in the unchanged survivor. Explicitly separate preparation or delivery, uncertainty or a failed challenge retains both rows. A semantic veto is not retried. Only a validated list certificate can discharge the operation difference; other conflicts remain active. Existing pair/cost limits, final-preservation checks, receipt validation and exact recovery apply.
