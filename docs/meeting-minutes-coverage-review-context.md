# Coverage review: honest guidance and usable source context

## Fixed reviewer-facing defects

Unbound coverage notices were using the generic missing-item template. It said the passage was not represented, although grouped materialization and timed-step coverage are uncertain. The agent now says coverage is uncertain and asks the reviewer to check the source before adding anything. Ordinary missing-item notices and directly linked field checks retain their existing behavior.

Coverage notices also offer a collapsed comparison list of current drafted actions sharing explicit, unambiguous source IDs. Each entry opens the actual current action. These are navigation links, not coverage judgments or warning targets. Different actions remain separate even when they share citations. The list explicitly states that shared sources do not establish coverage. It never adds, merges, edits, confirms or dismisses work. No published-action field error is inferred from shared evidence.

## Source provenance correction

Grouped-action materialization stores up to twenty source anchors, but the general warning normalizer reduced them to eight. Coverage-unresolved warnings now preserve the existing twenty-anchor budget; ordinary warnings keep eight.

The public draft projection can also recover the known historical truncation from its saved private audit. It requires the exact hashed group-notice ID, one matching unresolved issue and group record, the expected reason/type, identical ordered group/audit evidence, unique existing source IDs, and the warning's exact original eight-reference prefix. Conflicting, duplicate, missing, malformed or reviewer-authored provenance is left unchanged. Status, notes and action records are never changed. Private drafts are not mutated by projection. Audits which cannot establish this link remain limited to the recorded warning references.

## Saved-five qualification

Read-only replay of the last fresh five results preserves all actions, discussion paragraphs, flag counts and flag statuses. It recovers 72 recorded source references across 14 group notices: Abbott 27 references on five notices; DITA 19 on four; Allotment seven on one; Eakin 19 on four; Parking zero.

The final browser replay has 37 coverage notices with comparison lists (15 Abbott, five DITA, ten Allotment, seven Eakin), containing 81 navigation links. Parking correctly has none because it has no drafted actions. No coverage card asserts that its passage is absent. All five render without browser errors; production requests, provider calls and storage writes are prohibited during this replay. Grouped and timed-step warning counts remain honest.

## Remaining grouped-action work

All 30 accepted but unmapped group decisions remain unresolved. Their original verdicts do not contain complete source-backed atomic mappings. Three groups have dispositions for every listed child, but that neither proves complete group coverage nor resolves conflicting verdicts. No group is closed using shared source IDs, ownership overlap, individual child classification or a target alias.

The next implementation phase remains the explicit mapping contract described in [source-owner identities](meeting-minutes-source-owner-identities.md). Qualify individual obligation mapping, source-backed ownership and full retention, independent challenge, unresolved siblings, final save/reload and recovery against all 30 saved failures and separate-work negatives. Preserve the sparse-output guard. This release makes those notices easier and less misleading to review; it does not claim that semantic duplication or grouped coverage is fully fixed.
