# Meeting-minutes near-duplicate action bank

Working bank for AI-011 qualification. These examples are evidence records,
not permission to merge actions automatically. Each candidate must be checked
against its transcript source, owner, recipient, occurrence, timing,
conditions, warnings, reviewer history and save/export recovery.

## Disposition labels

- **Safe candidate:** same work occurrence is strongly evidenced; qualify a
  narrow source-aware consolidation.
- **Keep separate:** similar wording, but distinct scope, owner, recipient,
  timing, prerequisite or occurrence must remain visible.
- **Held / needs evidence:** repetition is visible, but the source or current
  generation behaviour is not sufficient to authorise a fix.
- **Verified existing safeguard:** a previously tested narrow case already has
  a bounded solution; retain it as a regression control.

## Current live pilot examples

### EAKIN-001 — GSOP review follow-up

- **Source:** Eakin T733 live draft 484, Actions stage, 26 October 2026.
- **Disposition:** Safe candidate.
- **Action A:** “Follow up on Louise's review of the GSOP procedure documentation.”
- **Action B:** “Follow up on the GSOP procedure review with Louise.”
- **Why it is near-duplicate:** Same GSOP review, same reviewer and same
  follow-up purpose; no distinct deadline or condition is added.
- **Preserve in the survivor:** Louise, GSOP/procedure context and the
  follow-up obligation.
- **Qualification:** Prove one row survives generation, save/reload and
  Word/PDF export without losing the source citation or recovery history.

### EAKIN-002 — request for document review after rewrites

- **Source:** Eakin T733 live draft 484, Actions stage, 26 October 2026.
- **Disposition:** Safe candidate.
- **Action A:** “Send an email requesting review of the revised documents after
  completing the rewrites and responses to feedback.”
- **Action B:** “Send an email requesting review after completing the document
  rewrites and responses to feedback comments.”
- **Why it is near-duplicate:** Same email/review occurrence and same
  prerequisite; the wording difference does not establish a different
  recipient or document set.
- **Preserve in the survivor:** Completion of rewrites/responses before the
  review request. Do not invent an email recipient.
- **Qualification:** Confirm source identity and recipient scope before any
  generic rule is attempted.

### EAKIN-003 — language-symbol update repetition (Discussion control)

- **Source:** Eakin T733 live draft 484, Discussion stage, 26 October 2026.
- **Disposition:** Safe candidate, but not an action rule.
- **Sentence A:** “Andrew is concurrently updating coding related to
  languages; four languages have minor symbol updates pending, and another
  four have more significant symbol issues to resolve before loading.”
- **Sentence B:** “Four items require minor symbolic/symbol updates before
  loading; four require consequential symbol resolution, ongoing this week and
  next week.”
- **Why it is near-duplicate:** Same source passage and same 4+4 split.
- **Preserve in the survivor:** Andrew, both language groups and the ongoing
  timing.
- **Qualification:** Keep this in the Discussion bank so it is not mistaken
  for an action-level merge.

## Historical saved-output examples

These are useful regression and adverse controls. Their saved-output
appearance is evidence of a candidate, not proof that current fresh generation
still reproduces it.

### SAVED-001 — marketing-plan drafting

- **Source:** Historical Eakin draft 467.
- **Disposition:** Safe candidate subject to fresh source replay.
- **Pattern:** Two marketing-plan drafting actions with the same owner and
  identical citations.
- **Preserve:** Any additional deliverable or scope; do not merge a broader
  row into a narrower row if the broader row adds work.
- **Control:** A broader deliverable must remain separate from a narrower
  drafting task.

- **Control:** Similar wording must not erase a timing constraint.

### SAVED-003 — webinar invitations

- **Source:** Historical Eakin draft 467.

### SAVED-006 — Colm and compilation pairs

- **Source:** Historical Eakin saved-pair replay.
- **Disposition:** Verified existing safeguard; retain as regression controls.
- **Pattern:** The previously tested Colm pair and compilation pair reduced the

### SAVED-007 — risk-management file update/share

- **Source:** Historical Eakin fresh output and saved draft 467 research.
- **Disposition:** Held / needs evidence.
- **Pattern:** Two visibly overlapping update/share rows with the same owner
  and shared source lines, but different Wednesday qualifications, warnings and
  rejected exact-date history.
- **Reason:** The rows may describe one occurrence, but the timing and warning
  metadata must not be lost. Existing semantic scopes intentionally reject this
  protected class.
- **Control:** Keep separate until a source-bound union can preserve every
  qualification and reviewer decision.

### LIBRARY-483-003 — action-bank adverse controls from the same draft

## Bank usage checklist

For every new pair, record:

1. transcript/case and stable source IDs;
2. exact action text for both rows;
3. owners, recipients, timing, conditions and warnings;
4. whether both rows describe the same work occurrence;
5. what information the survivor must retain;
6. an adverse control that must remain separate;
7. fresh-generation, save/reload, export and recovery results;
8. reviewer edits and observed burden in the AI-007 pilot.

The bank is complete enough to seed the first narrow AI-011 regression cases:
EAKIN-001, EAKIN-002, EAKIN-003, LIBRARY-483-001 and LIBRARY-483-002, with
SAVED-003, SAVED-005, SAVED-007 and LIBRARY-483-003 as separation controls.
