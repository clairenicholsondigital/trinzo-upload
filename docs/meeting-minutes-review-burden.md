# Bounded reviewer workload corrections

This batch changes only `/meeting-minutes-agent` timing accounting and retained-material presentation. It does not merge action rows, change Microsoft flows, delete drafts, clear caches, or automatically dismiss saved warnings.

## Descriptive retrospective timing

Discovery intentionally retains broad action candidates. A bare `is to`/`are to` can also nominate descriptions (`are to do with`, or a stated purpose/function/role/part of a process). Timed-step coverage previously treated that nomination as accepted timed work.

Coverage now excludes that narrow descriptive class only when:

- commitment is its sole recorded discovery cue;
- every detected timing expression is an explicit retrospective discussion reference, or `before` describing previous experience;
- reassessing the focus without the descriptive grammatical trigger establishes no independent commitment or acceptance.

The original inventory and source evidence remain intact. Calendar dates, future conditions, ambiguous timing, accepted requests, independent obligations and genuine assignments remain eligible. This does not solve every timing false positive and does not retire historic saved warnings.

Frozen latest-five replay: expected timed steps fall from 46 to 44 (Abbott 28→27; Eakin 9→8; the other three unchanged). Every surviving coverage record is identical apart from sequential display IDs. No actions, source records, reviewer decisions or retained entries are edited.

## Retained-material labels

Additional material is labelled optional and explicitly outside the main minutes. Warnings linked to supporting/unassigned records say `Not in the main minutes` and use `Open retained detail`. A warning linked to several retained entries is described as such, rather than as several actions. Published discussion/action targets retain their original labels and navigation.

All counts, sources, warning controls and filing/promotion behaviour remain. Optional means inclusion is optional; linked warnings still require checking before including the material. There is no new queue classification or leave-out disposition in this batch.

## Validation

Generic timing controls protect future assignments, conditional and overdue work, mixed descriptive/commitment passages, uncertain timing and independent cue types. Browser cases check attached, unassigned, ambiguous and shared published/retained warning destinations, and ensure navigation neither resolves warnings nor generates content. Existing filing/save/export and timing regression suites remain required.
