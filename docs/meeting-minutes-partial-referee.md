# Preserve checked discussion routes after partial referee failure

Previously, a failed referee batch could cause a topic-coverage fallback to replace the whole discussion with discovery. Valid supporting, merge and reject decisions elsewhere could consequently reappear as main paragraphs. Missing rows also activated the legacy supporting-to-core anchor repair and the unrestricted omission backstop.

Typed responses now retain each independently contract-valid decision. Missing, duplicated, malformed and invalid-target candidates remain eligible for fallback. A shared topic, source passage or similar sentence is never sufficient to suppress fallback. Discovery records are matched by complete record content and identity, ignoring only discovery disposition and primary-source navigation metadata. Different wording, evidence, warnings or unknown metadata remain recoverable. Reviewer content and records with descendants are not consumed by this matching.

For partial typed responses, unresolved discovery goes through the existing compacting/source checks and remains in main discussion, supporting context or Needs filing. Valid supporting records retain their target or remain unfiled; no parent is invented merely because another batch failed. The whole-discussion replacement remains available for legacy or wholly failed responses. Complete successful typed responses retain their existing path. Existing explicit material-support promotion settings remain respected.

The private quality state records the settled candidate IDs and exact discovery records routed by the preserved decisions. Supporting reconstruction also preserves warning metadata. Action generation, Microsoft prompts, similarity thresholds, existing drafts and other app pages are unchanged.

Validation includes generic positive/negative routing checks and saved-input assembly-boundary comparisons across five transcripts. These comparisons are not fresh model generations or a claim that all semantic repetition is fixed. In particular, separately generated variants with different wording remain eligible for recovery; combining partial overlaps still requires its own source-verified editor work.
