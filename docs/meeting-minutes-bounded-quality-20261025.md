# Bounded quality-state and candidate-availability fixes

Scope: Meeting Minutes Agent generation, its published payload and existing recovery/export boundaries. Existing library drafts are not rewritten.

## Closed mechanisms

- Completed correction annotations no longer act as unresolved warnings at the Discussion cleanup boundary. Supplied flag definitions now use the same existing usefulness policy as recovered definitions and publication. Genuine uncertainty, reviewer corrections and resolution notes remain protected. The shared reconciler's existing default is unchanged.
- A rejected non-temporal factual correction now leaves a persistent, typed source-review warning beside the retained original. Invalid replacements are not accepted, evidence thresholds are unchanged, and missing, malformed, stale or reviewer-authored targets are not reclassified as factual findings. Existing timing and commitment-strength warnings retain their separate handling.
- Actions now perform one synchronous ready-result check before freezing their candidate ensemble after an initial staged join timeout. Already completed results may join; pending, failed, expired or changed-input results cannot. Source content, prepared transcript, meeting type/date and inference policy stay bound to the result. No additional wait, extraction or provider retry is introduced. Later completion cannot mutate the frozen ensemble or reviewer work. Join status and used count are recorded privately.

## Evidence and closure limits

Synthetic regressions cover supplied/registry flag consistency, correction rejection across points/decisions/questions/supporting details, persistence, repeated application, manual edits, readiness before/after the initial timeout, completion after adjudication, failure, expiry and changed source/date/type/inference inputs.

Private saved-output replays cover five transcripts. The existing independently checked own-parent cleanup removes one demonstrated supporting-text repetition while retaining the parent's safety qualification; its evidence packet is unchanged and exact recovery succeeds. Publication and Word generation were checked. A captured low-support factual correction remains rejected, with its original now visibly warned through publication.

This closes the three mechanisms above. It does not close broad semantic repetition, automatically repair every rejected factual sentence, guarantee discovery of all actions, or reduce the wider filing backlog.

## Deliberately held out

A proposed narrow signing-duplicate nomination scope was tested privately. Both model checks agreed with the overlap, but the local ownership proof still rejected the returned witnesses. Both originals were retained. The experimental scope, feature flag and recovery changes are excluded from this release. Further assignment/acceptance context and proof-contract work is required; the guard was not weakened and the blocked verdict was not reinterpreted as approval.
