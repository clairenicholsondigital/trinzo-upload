## Corrections

1. Referee discovery selection runs once. A batch or repair preserves its selected identities instead of reclustering them, and its prompt uses the same copied, frozen contract as validation. A valid eight-row response and a seven-plus-one subset repair now reconcile against eight throughout.
2. A rival owner must support the action's operation. Needing a document, including a *completed* document, does not mean undertaking its preparation. A genuine obligation to complete or submit the work remains counter-evidence.
3. Document preparation/delivery merging compares document objects, preserves qualifying subjects and requires compatible document identity. Shared project words and recipients cannot connect unrelated documents or donate a deadline to them. Existing full-name and shortened-name hand-offs remain covered.
4. Commitment rechecking no longer treats a common owner/citation or loose wording similarity as proof of coverage. Exact, lossless coverage still suppresses redundant checking; uncertain paraphrases reach the existing quote check. That restores eligibility, not automatic publication.
5. Acknowledgements establish ownership only with an addressed request about the work. Explicit assignments and genuine first-person commitments retain their existing paths.
6. A merge/supporting-only group cannot reconstruct a main discussion point. Invalid primary references trigger the existing subset repair; the repair receives the retained primary's identity, wording and evidence separately from its counted candidate subset. Unresolved references remain unresolved if repair fails.
7. Final reconciliation recognises document-recording operations while earlier discovery and merging retain their established classification. Both dedupers prefer genuinely fuller detail over wording length. Final survival requires preservation of concrete details, including conditions, negative instructions, document modifiers and separate delivery/testing/review steps. It does not blindly trust a duplicate decision or removed ID.
