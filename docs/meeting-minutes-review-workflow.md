## Compilation safeguards

`MEETING_MINUTES_AGENT_COMPILATION_UNION_V1=1` is captured in the semantic policy. The new path runs only when the existing workflow editor has no eligible candidate, preserving its priority and budget. It admits one pair of generated single-instruction Compile actions with the same owner and related, substantially overlapping citations. Dates, conflicting quantities/dependencies, reviewer edits/keeps, rejected timing history, unknown metadata and separate operations are excluded.

Instruction truth and complete claim retention require inventory, proposal and independent verification; a fourth assessment verifies one outstanding work event with a source quote tying the work to its owner. Timing metadata is not part of this source judgement. It remains unchanged, with an unresolved binding still unresolved. Source/date/content/metadata-bound receipts authorize the final survivor; changed input or failed checks retain both originals. Exact whole-register recovery is disabled after unrelated edits or keeps. Restoring a compilation leaves earlier contact reconciliation independently recoverable.

## Filing identity and reviewer exclusion

`MEETING_MINUTES_AGENT_CITED_FILING_ALIASES_V1=1` is captured in the discussion policy. A candidate's preserved generated record must name the unique original explicitly. Citation unions up to 24 are retained on optional filing records, without changing generated main-record evidence limits. Explicit reviewer filing/promotion preserves all those references. Original records, origins and a complete bounded recovery audit remain; unmatched Abbott repeats are not deleted by text equality.

Leave-out records are server-owned. The browser sends selected fingerprints; it cannot replace archived source/history through that selection. Fingerprints include full record metadata and the cited text/speaker/inclusion state, so IDs alone or a changed source cannot exclude another occurrence. The active pool excludes exact handled occurrences; archives are excluded from main minutes and exports, remain accessible and keep their warnings. No warning resolution is implied by exclusion. New wording, citations or provenance may require a new decision.

## Ceiling and next validation

This closes the bounded mechanisms in the task list, not all possible semantic repetition or all provider coverage limitations. Distinct-looking/ambiguous work, unsupported original assertions, missing/overlarge source context and reviewer-protected content remain untouched. Reviewer exclusion is a preference, not a judgement that the information is unimportant. Coverage notices do not establish missing actions, and fewer visible cards do not establish factual completeness.
