Beta discussion: flat items with operative qualifications

New Beta generations can use a Microsoft discussion specialist that produces one
list of discussion items. Each item keeps its outcome, reasoning, uncertainty
and operative conditions together, rather than restating the same proposition
under discussion, decisions and open questions. Decisions remain in prose.

Architecture: unchanged preparation and three concurrent specialist requests.
The application validates the flat contract and places its exact items in one
heading-hidden storage container for existing editing, publication and Word
export. This adapter neither rewrites nor merges generated content. Provider
responses remain verbatim in branch diagnostics. Grouped replies and saved
older drafts remain supported. No action or summary instructions change.

Activation: discussion.format = flat-v1 in the private specialist configuration
selects config/meeting-minutes-parallel/discussion-flat.txt for instruction-hash
verification. Deploy compatible code first, then atomically change only the
private discussion configuration to the verified published flat agent/flow.
Retain the previous private configuration for rollback; it selects the original
discussion.txt. Never commit signed webhook URLs. Configuration is read per run.

Qualification (2026-10-28): 20 fresh direct discussion calls, ten matched pairs
across six existing corpus transcripts. Four cases had two repeats per version;
two additional cases had one repeat per version. AI source-led editor judgement
preferred flat in eight pairs, judged two similar; repetition was cleaner in
seven and similar in three. No confirmed new material factual contradiction was
identified, with precision cautions and supporting omissions still recorded.
The previous explicit-deferral regression was retained in both revised repeats
and an additional complete three-agent run. Mean discussion call time was 12.7s
flat versus 15.2s current; mean discussion text 545 versus 519 words. These are
discussion-only metrics, not full-workflow time or measured human editing time.
Two complete three-agent runs, exact publication/export preservation, and actual
case-output desktop/mobile rendering were also checked outside production.

This is a bounded improvement on familiar examples, not proof of universal
quality. Corpus provenance was not independently verified. Role labels,
historical assumptions and conditional audit-finding phrasing remain editorial
precision cautions. Shared preparation losses and separate action/summary
weaknesses are not addressed by this change. Archived evaluation remains on the
VPS at /root/trinzo-flat-discussion-v2-20261028, including source-linked pair
judgements, raw wire responses, exact requests and timings.
