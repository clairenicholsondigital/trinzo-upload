# Supporting paragraph coverage by its own parent

The final discussion pipeline can retain a shorter version underneath a fuller
paragraph after late organisation or demotion. The earlier literal cleanup
does not establish whether a semantic paraphrase is redundant.

The optional `MEETING_MINUTES_AGENT_DISCUSSION_OWN_PARENT_COVERAGE_V1` pass runs
after source reconciliation and the existing bounded editor. It defaults off;
deployment may explicitly enable it after isolated validation.

## Bounds and proof

Only generated leaf children of their own points or decisions are eligible.
Reviewer content, child warnings, unknown metadata, simulations, new citations,
new primary citations, incompatible dispositions, duplicate identities/sources
and overflowing history veto nomination. A warning on the unchanged parent
stays on that parent. Questions and cross-parent comparisons are excluded.

At most three children, one per parent, are assessed. Lexical overlap nominates
only. Source context includes recorded references and their immediate neighbours,
bounded to 24 units and 48,000 serialized characters. No evidence is donated.

The existing Microsoft correction-check service must inventory every child
assertion with exact original, parent and source quotes. An independent call
checks every inventory claim and every original child sentence for complete
inventory, source support and unchanged-parent retention. Any false check,
missing check, malformed/duplicate response, invented quote, timeout or changed
input preserves the child.

Coverage is directional: the parent may retain additional non-conflicting
source-supported detail. Parent quotes identify the relevant unchanged span;
the assessor must also inspect the full parent for contradictions. Existing
name, figure, polarity, modality, condition, phase and temporal rejection guards
are applied to the mapped spans. No global merge or timing guard is relaxed.
A positive model answer is necessary but never overrides a deterministic veto.

## Preservation and recovery

Only the covered child occurrence is removed. Parent prose, evidence,
primary evidence and warning links remain unchanged. Child identity and full
merge history are retained without truncation; complete originals, inventory
and independent checks live in the private quality-state archive.

The existing owned, revision-guarded Restore combined paragraphs operation
restores affected topics. If the previous bounded editor already changed
discussion, archives are composed only across an exact source-identical chain.
Both sets of originals/proofs remain recoverable; an invalid chain keeps the
previous result. No-op saves preserve recovery, reviewer edits invalidate stale
recovery, and unrelated topic edits survive restoration.

Existing library drafts are not migrated or rewritten by deployment. New
generation uses the enabled pass. Ordinary exports already omit supporting
context; evidence-enabled exports reflect the removed child in their appendix.

## Validation and remaining limits

Regression tests exercise malformed and negative contracts, metadata limits,
stale calls, warning/reviewer exclusions, archive composition and actual
PATCH/GET/restore/undo/redo and Word/PDF paths. Isolated real-data replays compare
all five saved drafts and preserve the library; real Microsoft negative controls
exercise unique quality, vehicle count, attribution, timing, recipient,
condition, amount, uncertainty, decision status and purpose.

The initial visible target is an entirely covered repair paragraph beneath
its fuller parent. Unique travel details, quality/purpose qualifiers and
source-supported child context must remain. Warning-bearing children (including
confidentiality and fee examples) require a separate claim-scoped warning
design. Rewriting a partially redundant child is also outside this pass.
This is a bounded reduction of repetition, not a claim that all repetition is
fixed or that probabilistic assessments are infallible.
