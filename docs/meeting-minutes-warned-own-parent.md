# Warning-preserving own-parent coverage

`MEETING_MINUTES_AGENT_WARNED_OWN_PARENT_V1` is separately captured for new agent
runs. Legacy/resumed work cannot adopt it. Existing unflagged nomination retains
priority and the total three-candidate cap; at most one warned child is nominated.

Only near-verbatim generated leaf children of their unchanged existing parent
qualify. Their complete, unique, open timing-warning definitions must match known
reference-binding or quote-uniqueness failures and cited source IDs. Unknown
metadata, corrected/reviewer warnings, unsupported facts, qualification failures,
missing definitions and changed sources remain protected. Similarity nominates;
it never authorizes deletion.

Both source inventory and independent challenge must prove every child assertion,
full sentence and qualification supported and retained. The full warning registry
is bound against edits during assessment. Only literal case changes in existing
name tokens are accepted on this new path; token identity and order remain
protected. This handles sentence-initial ordinary words without fuzzy name
matching. Default shared/legacy comparison behaviour stays unchanged.

An admitted deletion keeps parent wording and source references unchanged, adds
all child warning IDs to the parent without editing their definitions/status,
and archives the full original child, parent, warning definitions and proofs.
Topic-scoped recovery restores the exact original content and links. It never
clears or treats warning transfer as correction of an unsupported statement.

Private qualification on five frozen outputs removes one proven document-access
restatement and preserves its open warning. Another stronger required assertion
is declined. JSON/public projection, Word/PDF warning visibility and exact restore
are checked without modifying library drafts. Earlier failed qualification and
its capitalization cause are retained in private evidence.
