# Scoped supporting-detail subjects

The private five-draft trace reproduced an unassessed technical detail attaching
to a travel parent through “upon”, “arrival” and “site”. The body matcher treated
those incidental words as the subject even though the technical identifier was
absent. This was a fallback match, not a Microsoft target decision.

`MEETING_MINUTES_AGENT_SUPPORTING_SUBJECT_V2` separately captures an agent-only
subject policy. Incidental acquisition/location words no longer establish a
subject. The existing short-technical-identifier rule can then select the unique
actual technical body. Explicit target identity is also checked for positive
subject support under this policy. Unresolved/ambiguous cases remain losslessly
retained; reviewer-authored destinations, parents and subtrees remain protected.
Legacy callers and captured runs without this setting retain their prior policy.

The scope is the agent's compaction and organiser/reorganisation calls. The
private frozen rehoming comparison moves the misplaced warned detail to document
access and preserves its full text, source IDs, warning, identity and original
topic. Comparing old and new passes over the same five bodies adds no filing
entries; all original children are preserved. This does not assert that all
existing filing material has a safe destination.
